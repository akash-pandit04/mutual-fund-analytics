## 1. Stock Market Fundamentals Overview

### 1.1 Stock Exchanges: NSE & BSE
- **National Stock Exchange (NSE):** Founded in 1992, NSE is India's leading stock exchange by volume. Its flagship index is the **Nifty 50**.
- **Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE):** Established in 1875, BSE is Asia's oldest stock exchange. Its flagship index is the **Sensex** (30 stocks).

### 1.2 Initial Public Offering (IPO)
An IPO is the process by which a private company issues shares to the public for the first time to raise capital. It transforms a private company into a publicly traded entity listed on exchanges.

### 1.3 Key Valuation & Financial Metrics
- **Market Capitalization:** Total dollar/rupee value of a company's outstanding shares ($\text{Market Cap} = \text{Shares Outstanding} \times \text{Current Stock Price}$).
- **Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio:** Evaluates valuation relative to earnings ($\text{P/E} = \frac{\text{Price per Share}}{\text{Earnings per Share (EPS)}}$).
- **Price-to-Book (P/B) Ratio:** Compares market value to book value ($\text{P/B} = \frac{\text{Market Price per Share}}{\text{Book Value per Share}}$).
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Company's net profit divided by total outstanding shares.
- **Dividend Yield:** Percentage of stock price paid out in annual dividends.

### 1.4 Financial Statements Summary
1. **Balance Sheet:** Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity. Highlights liquidity and capital structure.
2. **Profit & Loss (P&L):** Revenue, Operating Expenses, Operating Profit (EBITDA), Net Profit (PAT).
3. **Cash Flow Statement:** Cash from Operating Activities (CFO), Investing Activities (CapEx), and Financing Activities.
