## 1. FinTech Landscape & Trading Lifecycle

### 1.1 Key Stakeholders & Regulators
- **SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India):** Primary regulatory body ensuring market integrity and investor protection.
- **Depositories (NSDL & CDSL):** Hold shares in electronic dematerialized (Demat) form.
- **Clearing Corporations (NSCCL / ICCL):** Guarantee settlement of all executed trades.

### 1.2 Trading Lifecycle (T+1 Settlement)
1. **Order Placement:** Client places order on platform (Kite/Growth/Upstox).
2. **Risk Check & Routing:** Order validation by broker OMS (Order Management System) and submission to Exchange (NSE/BSE).
3. **Trade Execution:** Matching buyers and sellers at exchange.
4. **Clearing & Settlement:** T+1 day funds payout and securities transfer to Demat account.

## 2. FinTech Platform Case Study: Zerodha

### 2.1 Business & Revenue Model
- **Zero Brokerage on Equity Delivery:** Attracts retail investors.
- **Flat ₹20 per Trade on F&O & Intraday:** Primary revenue driver.
- **Kite Connect API:** Monetized API ecosystem for algorithmic traders.

### 2.2 Data Analytics Applications
- **Nudge Mechanism:** Real-time behavioral analytics alerting users against high-risk trades (e.g., illiquid options).
- **Console Analytics:** P&L heatmap, tax reports, and portfolio analytics.
